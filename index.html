🔑 Why is the "Create" button missing in Google?

If you went to Google App Passwords and didn't see a "Create" button or got "This setting is not available":

  1. 2-Step Verification is turned OFF: Google hides the Create button until 2-Step Verification is activated.

  2. Search in Google Account: Open myaccount.google.com, and in the top search bar type App passwords. Click on it from the search results.
  3. Work / College / School Accounts: If your email ends in .edu or belongs to an organization, Google disables App Passwords. Use a personal @gmail.com account instead.
